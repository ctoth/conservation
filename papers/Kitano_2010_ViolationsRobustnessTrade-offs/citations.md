## Key Citations for Follow-up

- Bode (1945): the sensitivity integral Kitano limits to "conservation of the sensitivities of negative feedback circuits on a frequency axis" (p.1).
- Csete & Doyle (2002): source of the "robust yet fragile" trade-off claim Kitano conditions on optimality (p.1).
- Carlson & Doyle (1999); Reynolds, Carlson & Doyle (2002): HOT, the optimization-produces-fragility mechanism Kitano links to portfolio diversity (Fig. 1 caption, p.3).
- Kitano (2007): the earlier paper asking whether the robustness/fragility trade-off is a conservation principle (already in collection).
- Ibarra, Edwards & Palsson (2002): the experimental evolution data Kitano reads as convergence to the frontier (p.6).
