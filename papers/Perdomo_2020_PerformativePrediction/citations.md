## Key Citations for Follow-up

- [15] Hardt et al. 2016, Strategic Classification: the original Stackelberg formulation this paper subsumes; agents gaming a classifier is the direct Goodhart case.
- [32] Miller, Milli, Hardt 2019: distinguishes gaming from improvement causally, which is the proxy-vs-true-quality split this paper does not model.
- [25] Kleinberg & Raghavan 2019: which classifiers induce effort on the true target vs on the proxy.
- [13] Fournier & Guillin 2015: W1 concentration used for the finite-sample theorem.
- [37] Shavit, Edelman, Axelrod 2020: accuracy vs improvement vs causality under strategic agents.
