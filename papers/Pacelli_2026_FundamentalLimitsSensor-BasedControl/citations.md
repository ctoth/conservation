## Key Citations for Follow-up

- [5], [6] Majumdar, (Mei,) Pacelli: the TRIP bound. These are the prior bounds the paper says evaluate sensor MI under the open-loop/uncontrolled state distribution; Remark 1 shows they are the β → ∞, indicator-cost special case of this paper's bound.
- [7] Gerchinovitz, Ménard, Stoltz: the generalized Fano inequality underlying TRIP.
- [2] Bode and [3] Nair et al.: the classical problem-class-specific limits the paper positions against; directly relevant to a Bode-type-limit study.
- [14] Chen, Georgiou, Tannenbaum: thermodynamic fundamental limits of stochastic control.
- [20] Sagawa & Ueda: feedback-control second law; the authors flag a structural parallel as future work.
