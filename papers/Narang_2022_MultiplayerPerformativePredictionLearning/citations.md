## Key Citations for Follow-up

- Perdomo et al. 2020: single-player performative prediction; defines performative stability vs optimality.
- Miller, Perdomo, Zrnic 2021: convexity of performative risk; this paper's Theorem 5 reduces to their Theorem 3.1 at n = 1.
- Drusvyatskiy & Xiao 2020: decision-dependent SGD as static SGD with vanishing bias; the analysis template used throughout.
- Rosen 1965: strong monotonicity gives a unique Nash equilibrium.
- Drusvyatskiy, Fazel, Ratliff 2021: derivative-free gradient play rates used for Proposition 1.
