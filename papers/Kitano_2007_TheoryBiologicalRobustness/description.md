Kitano's 2007 editorial defines biological robustness as a probability-weighted integral of relative function preservation over a perturbation space and distinguishes it from stability and homeostasis. It poses, without proof, that over the entire (or a sufficiently broad) perturbation space with equiprobable weighting, the robustness difference between two tunings of the same system is zero, by analogy with the Bode sensitivity integral, MCA summation theorems, HOT, and the gain-bandwidth product, and leaves open whether such conservation exists "in either relative or strict manner". It is the primary source for the "conservation of robustness" framing whose conditions this project is checking.
